Our Story

Born on Mexico's Pacific Coast

Sinalove is more than a restaurant — it's a piece of home brought to Guelph. Owners Erendira and Chef Joel Romero arrived from Sinaloa, Mexico, carrying with them the bold, ocean-fresh traditions of Mexico's Pacific coast.

Every dish is crafted from recipes rooted in Mazatlán and the Sinaloan coastline — from raw aguachiles marinated in fresh lime and chile, to birria slow-cooked until it falls apart, to sushi rolls that fuse the flavours of coastal Mexico with Japanese technique.

When they renamed their restaurant Sinalove, it was a declaration: this place is made with love, and every bite should feel like it.
