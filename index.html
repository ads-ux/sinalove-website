Our Story

Born on Mexico's Pacific Coast

Sinalove is more than a restaurant — it's a piece of home brought to Guelph. Owners Erendira and Chef Joel Romero arrived from Sinaloa, Mexico's Pacific coast state, carrying the bold, ocean-fresh culinary traditions of Mazatlán.

Every dish is crafted from recipes rooted in the Sinaloan coastline — from raw aguachiles marinated in fresh lime and chile, to birria slow-cooked until it falls apart, to sushi rolls that fuse the flavours of coastal Mexico with Japanese technique.

When they named their restaurant Sinalove, it was a declaration: this place is made with love, and every bite should feel like it.

Common Questions

FAQ

What type of food does Sinalove serve?

Sinalove serves authentic Sinaloan Mexican cuisine from Mexico's Pacific coast — birria tacos, fresh aguachiles, ceviches, Sinaloan-style sushi rolls, seafood cocktails, and a weekend brunch featuring traditional café de olla.

Is Sinalove halal?

Yes. Sinalove uses halal-certified beef and chicken. Halal items include the birria tacos, Taco de Arrachera, and related quesadillas.

Does Sinalove serve brunch?

Yes — every Saturday and Sunday, 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Brunch features authentic Sinaloan Mexican breakfast and traditional café de olla (clay pot coffee).

Where is Sinalove in Guelph?

Sinalove is at 447 Elizabeth Street, Guelph, Ontario N1E 6C2. Closed Mondays. Open Tuesday–Sunday, with weekend brunch on Saturday and Sunday mornings.

Does Sinalove have vegan options?

Yes. The vegan menu includes the Veggi Roll, Zucchini Taco, Mushroom Taco, and Guacamole & Chips. Many ceviches and sushi can also be adapted.

Can I order Sinalove for pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order directly for pickup or delivery at sinalovemexican.bycalibre.ca, or through Uber Eats.
